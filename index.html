GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a stay?

Open the booking form, fill in your details, preferred check-in and check-out dates, and number of guests, then submit. The camp will review your request and get back to you.

Is my booking confirmed right away?

No. Every booking request is subject to confirmation by the camp. You will be contacted once your dates and details have been reviewed.

Can I bring a group?

Yes. The camp welcomes expedition groups and group stays. Include your number of guests in the booking form so the camp can prepare.

What activities are available?

Beach exploration and island adventures are available. Activities depend on weather conditions and arrangements with the camp, so ask about them in your inquiry.

How much are the rates?

Rates and availability must be verified with the camp. The booking form shows a summary of your selected options before you submit.

How do I get to Masinlo Beach Camp?

The camp is in Sitio Asis, Barangay Maroyogroyog, Linapacan, Palawan. Send an inquiry and the camp can advise you on the best way to get there.

What if the weather is bad on my travel dates?

Weather can affect travel and activities in Palawan. Contact the camp as early as possible so your plans can be adjusted.

Guest Guidelines

Please read these before you book your stay.

Booking Policy

  • Booking requests are subject to confirmation by the camp.
  • Your reservation is not final until the camp confirms it.
  • No online payment is required when you submit a request.

Guest Capacity

  • The minimum and maximum number of guests is shown in the booking form.
  • Extra tents can be requested for bigger groups.

Cancellation & Changes

  • Need to change or cancel? Contact the camp as early as possible.
  • Cancellation terms are confirmed by the camp when your booking is reviewed.

Safety & Camp Rules

  • Quiet hours: 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
  • Clean as you go. Waste segregation is strictly enforced.
  • The camp is not liable for lost personal items or accidents caused by guest negligence.
  • Follow the safety guidelines for each activity.
